About the Game

Bubble Shooter - Rescue Pandas builds on classic bubble shooting by introducing a simple rescue objective.
Instead of just clearing the board, players focus on freeing pandas trapped inside bubble clusters.
This adds a sense of purpose to every move. Each successful shot doesn’t just clear space — it helps progress toward saving all characters on the level.

Gameplay Mechanics

  • Aim and Match

    Players shoot bubbles to match colors and clear clusters. You'll need to think a few shots ahead if you want to get to those pandas efficiently.

  • Rescue Objective

    Pandas are positioned within or beneath bubble formations. Clearing surrounding bubbles releases them, often triggering satisfying chain reactions.

Key Features

It's the kind of game where you can chill out but still feel like you're accomplishing something.

  • Classic bubble shooter mechanics

  • Rescue-based level objectives

  • Increasingly complex layouts

  • Simple and intuitive controls

  • Emotion-driven gameplay

Level Design

Each level is basically a little rescue mission with its own setup. Early stages introduce simple setups, while later ones require more precise shots and planning to reach hidden pandas. You'll find yourself holding your breath when a panda's almost free, then celebrating when they finally drop down.
